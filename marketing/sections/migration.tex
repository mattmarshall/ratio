\section{An Open Ecosystem, and a Path Off Legacy}

Ratio is not a monolith you must adopt wholesale. It is an ecosystem of composable
parts around a proven core, each speaking open formats and a standard API. That
makes it adoptable incrementally --- and reversible, which is precisely the
property lock-in is designed to deny you.

\subsection{A staged path}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Shadow.} Run Ratio alongside your incumbent, replaying the same
  transaction feed. Because Ratio is deterministic and exact, any discrepancy is a
  precise, reproducible signal --- an instant, ongoing reconciliation against your
  current system.
  \item \textbf{Verify.} Use Ratio as the independent source of truth for audit,
  restatement, and ``as-of'' reporting, where reproducibility and proof matter most.
  \item \textbf{Adopt.} Move reporting, client, and integration surfaces onto the
  open API at your pace, with your data in formats you control the whole way.
\end{itemize}

\noindent Because the core is open and self-hostable, your firm's accounting truth
never again lives somewhere you cannot read it, reproduce it, or take it with you.
