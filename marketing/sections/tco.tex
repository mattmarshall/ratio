\section{The Economics of Certainty}

Incumbent platforms typically price as a function of assets under management. That
couples your technology bill to your investment success --- a tax on growth that has
nothing to do with the cost of serving you. Layered on top are implementation fees,
per-seat add-ons, and the soft cost of the operations headcount that exists to
reconcile a system you cannot verify.

Ratio inverts the model. Pricing on usage --- not on AUM --- means your platform
cost flattens as your book grows, and exact, reproducible accounting shrinks the
reconciliation burden rather than feeding it.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cost decoupled from AUM.} Doubling your book does not double your
  platform bill.
  \item \textbf{Lower operational drag.} Exactness and bit-exact replay remove
  whole categories of reconciliation and restatement work.
  \item \textbf{No lock-in premium.} Open data formats and a complete export ---
  every entry, every rule, every configuration hash --- mean your exit cost, and
  therefore your negotiating leverage, is fundamentally different.
\end{itemize}
