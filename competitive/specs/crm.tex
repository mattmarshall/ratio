\spechead{Client Relationship Management (CRM)}{crm}

Contact, household, and activity management wired into reporting and trading so
advisors act on portfolio context.

\specsection{Advertised capabilities (feature level)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Contact and household relationship management.
  \item Activity, task, and workflow tracking; calendar and pipeline.
  \item Tight integration with performance reporting and rebalancing tools.
  \item Surfacing of portfolio data (performance, alerts) in the client record.
\end{itemize}

\begin{ratiobox}
\textbf{Ratio mapping.} CRM is an \emph{extension-tier} application (Python
extension surface) that consumes the gRPC \texttt{Ledger} API for portfolio
context. It holds relationship state, not financial truth; it never writes ledger
entries directly. Household structure aligns with the ledger's book/entity scoping
so CRM views and accounting views agree by construction.
\end{ratiobox}

\specsection{Conservation \& trust placement}
\textbf{Extension tier, outside the trusted core.} Relationship data store;
read-only against the ledger.
