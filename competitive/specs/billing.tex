\specsection{Advertised capabilities (feature level)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Configurable fee schedules: tiered, flat, and AUM-based; householding rules.
  \item Automated fee calculation and invoice generation.
  \item Revenue management and practice-level revenue analytics.
  \item Fee reconciliation against centralized, aggregated data.
\end{itemize}

\begin{ratiobox}
\textbf{Ratio mapping.} Fee schedules are \emph{control-plane} policy; a billing
run \emph{proposes} fee transactions that the kernel admits only if balanced
(fee debit $=$ revenue credit), and every fee entry references the
content-addressed schedule version that produced it --- so any invoice is exactly
reproducible and auditable. Notably, Ratio's own \emph{platform} pricing is
decoupled from client AUM (see the positioning brief); this component bills
\emph{your} clients, not you.
\end{ratiobox}

\specsection{Conservation \& trust placement}
\textbf{Control plane (proposes) + trusted core (admits + records).} Fee math is
integer-exact; no rounding drift across invoices.
