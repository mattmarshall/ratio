\begin{ratiobox}
\textbf{Ratio mapping.} This is where Ratio's architecture is categorically
safer. LLMs sit \emph{above the trust boundary} as an authoring copilot: they
translate intent into typed specifications and draft explanations, but every
proposal is compiled and checked against the kernel's invariants before it can
touch a ledger. Bolting AI onto a black-box ledger gives a confident model write
access to truth; Ratio gives it propose-only access. A hallucination is a
discarded suggestion, never a corrupted book.
\end{ratiobox}

\specsection{Conservation \& trust placement}
\textbf{Authoring tier, strictly outside the trusted core.} Propose-only; all
outputs pass the deterministic compiler + conservation check.
