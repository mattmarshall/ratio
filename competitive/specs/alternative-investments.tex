\begin{ratiobox}
\textbf{Ratio mapping.} Alts are first-class in Ratio's model: each private
position, commitment, and unfunded balance is just another \emph{conserved
dimension} in the integer-vector basis, so a capital call is one balanced
transaction spanning cash, commitment, and position dimensions --- no bolt-on
sub-ledger. Valuations and benchmarks enter as typed facts with provenance.
\end{ratiobox}

\specsection{Conservation \& trust placement}
\textbf{Trusted core (as additional dimensions) + fact plane (valuations).}
The conservation law generalizes to alts at no structural cost.
