\begin{ratiobox}
\textbf{Ratio mapping.} Each specialized line is a \emph{control-plane} policy
package (posting rules, account mappings) plus, where needed, additional conserved
dimensions in the kernel basis --- the same generalization that absorbs alts.
Trust, retirement, and insurance servicing become configurations over one proven
ledger, not separate sub-systems with their own correctness story.
\end{ratiobox}

\specsection{Conservation \& trust placement}

without a new engine per asset line.
