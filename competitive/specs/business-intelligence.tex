\begin{ratiobox}

aggregations of the proven ledger plus CRM/extension data. Because the underlying
ledger is exact and replayable, firm metrics are reproducible as-of any date ---
``what was our AUM and revenue on this date, under that fee policy'' is a
re-run, not an estimate. No trusted-core involvement; pure read-side projection.
