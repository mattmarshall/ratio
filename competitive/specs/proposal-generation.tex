\begin{ratiobox}
\textbf{Ratio mapping.} A proposal is a \emph{simulation}: candidate transactions
run against a sandboxed copy of the proven kernel, so the projected post-trade
state and tax impact are computed by the same exact engine that will book the
real trades --- the proposal and the eventual reality cannot diverge by
construction. The AI authoring copilot can draft the narrative; the numbers come
from the kernel.
\end{ratiobox}

\specsection{Conservation \& trust placement}

the kernel read-only for projections.
