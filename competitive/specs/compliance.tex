\specsection{Advertised capabilities (feature level)}

  \item Ongoing surveillance / monitoring with flagging for review.
  \item Exam readiness; books-and-records retention.
  \item Configurable rule authoring; audit trails of checks and overrides.
\end{itemize}

\begin{ratiobox}
\textbf{Ratio mapping.} Compliance rules are \emph{control-plane} predicates
evaluated on proposed transactions before admission --- structurally the same
``propose, then check'' seam as conservation, so a violating trade is rejected at
the boundary. Critically, Ratio's append-only, content-addressed ledger \emph{is}
the books-and-records substrate: every check, override, and rule version is itself
a provenance-bearing, replayable record. ``Prove what the rule was and that it
ran'' is a deterministic query.
\end{ratiobox}

\specsection{Conservation \& trust placement}
\textbf{Control plane (rules) + trusted core (immutable record).} Surveillance
reads the proven log; the log's immutability is the audit guarantee.
